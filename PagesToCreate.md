📋 Remaining Essential Pages for Electronics E-commerce:
Based on standard e-commerce best practices, here are the key pages you still need to create:

Product-Related Pages:
    Product Detail Page - Individual product view with images, specs, reviews
    Category Pages - Electronics categories (Laptops, Smartphones, Accessories, etc.)
    Search Results Page - Advanced search with filters
    Compare Products Page - Side-by-side product comparison
User Account Pages:
    User Dashboard - Account overview, order history
    Order History - Past orders with tracking
    Wishlist - Saved products
    Profile Settings - Account management
    Address Book - Shipping/billing addresses
Support & Information Pages:
    About Us - Company information
    Contact Us - Contact form and information
    FAQ - Frequently asked questions
    Shipping & Returns - Policies
    Privacy Policy - Privacy terms
    Terms of Service - Usage terms
    Size Guide - For accessories (if applicable)
Specialty Electronics Pages:
    Brand Pages - Apple, Samsung, Sony, etc.
    New Arrivals - Latest products
    Best Sellers - Popular products
    Clearance/Sale - Discounted items
    Pre-order - Upcoming products
Order & Transaction Pages:
    Order Confirmation - Post-purchase confirmation
    Order Tracking - Track shipment status
    Payment Methods - Available payment options
    Gift Cards - Gift card purchase and balance
